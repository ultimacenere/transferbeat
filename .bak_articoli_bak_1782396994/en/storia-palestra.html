Palestra to Chelsea, here we go: Inter beaten at the death

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 24 Jun 2026

Marco Palestra is heading to the Premier League: according to Calciomercato.com it is «here we go» for his move to Chelsea, with a verbal agreement reached with Atalanta «for a total of more than €55 million». Inter, who had chased him for weeks and considered the deal all but done, are left empty-handed: the player has chosen Xabi Alonso's side.

The breakthrough came on Wednesday morning. Calciomercato.com announced «Marco Palestra – Chelsea, here we go!», reporting a verbal agreement with Atalanta «for a total of more than €55 million». A fee that sends the young Atalanta-owned full-back to the Premier League after a season on loan at Cagliari.

It is Chelsea who came out on top, overtaking Inter in the final hours. La Gazzetta dello Sport wrote that «Palestra says yes to Chelsea», with Inter «caught off guard» and the player set to earn around €6 million a season; Sky Sport, the most authoritative source on the matter, had already reported the London club «close to an agreement with Palestra and Atalanta».

On how it happened, Corriere della Sera reconstructed the snub: the player was reportedly won over by «the meeting with his agent» and «the video call with Xabi Alonso», the Chelsea manager. Eurosport summed up the day bluntly: «Palestra, no Inter: he has accepted Chelsea's offer».

Yet only a few days ago the move seemed set in the opposite direction. In mid-June Eurosport had revealed a verbal agreement between Inter and Atalanta, while between 21 and 22 June Sky Sport called the deal «in its closing stages» and Quotidiano Sportivo had Inter «one step away». For weeks, La Gazzetta reported, Palestra himself had preferred Inter to the calls of Manchester City and Newcastle.

The operation now has to be formalised: the reported agreement is verbal, and the official confirmation and the final details on bonuses and structure are still missing. Meanwhile Inter, La Gazzetta writes, «after Palestra's no» have switched decisively to other targets, from Jones to Solet, to strengthen the flank.
