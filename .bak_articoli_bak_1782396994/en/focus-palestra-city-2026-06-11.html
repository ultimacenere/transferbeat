Palestra to Chelsea: a €57m agreement, Inter snubbed

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 25 Jun 2026

A final twist in the Palestra saga: in the end the Premier League won. Overnight, Chelsea and Atalanta reached an agreement in principle worth €57m plus €3m in bonuses and 10% of any future resale. The 2005-born wing-back gets a five-year deal; Inter, long in pole position, are left empty-handed.

The deal is in the home straight. According to reports, Chelsea and Atalanta found their agreement overnight between 23 and 24 June: €57m plus €3m in bonuses, with a further 10% sell-on clause in Atalanta's favour. A total outlay close to €60m, making Palestra one of the most lucrative sales in the Bergamo club's history.

A five-year contract is ready for Marco Palestra, worth around €5m a season. The decisive factor, they say, was a direct chat with Xabi Alonso: the Chelsea manager laid out the role the youngster would have in the new project, convincing him to choose London.

It is a blow for Inter, who for weeks had made the right flank a priority and Palestra their number-one target, going as far as raising their bid to €50m. But Chelsea's financial muscle and Premier League pressure flipped a duel that looked headed for Milan. In the end, the decision was the player's.

Only the final bureaucratic steps and the signatures remain before it becomes official, which is expected shortly. Barring a dramatic U-turn, Marco Palestra is a new Chelsea player.
