Solet-Inter at the final details, Dibu-Juve: June 10 speeds up the market

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 10 Jun 2026

Inter close in on Oumar Solet from Udinese in a deal worth around 25 million, while Juventus secure an agreement with Emiliano “Dibu” Martinez. A busy day for Milan and Roma too, with Glasner edging closer to the Rossoneri bench and Bastoni's agent shutting down exit talk.

Chivu's Inter are flexing their muscles on the Udinese front. Oumar Solet is getting closer: according to Calciomercato.com it is a deal worth around 25 million, and Sky Sport confirms the gap with the Friulians has narrowed considerably. Midfielder Arthur Atta was also part of the summit, and in midfield the Nerazzurri are weighing up Atta against Liverpool's Curtis Jones, with Calciomercato.com analysing the pros and cons of both. For the goalkeeper spot, the trail leading to Guglielmo Vicario from Tottenham remains hot, while Sky Sport reports on the meeting between Inter and the agent of Lazio's Ivan Provedel.

On the outgoing front, the names of Calhanoglu, linked with Fenerbahçe, plus Bisseck and Luis Henrique are circulating. On Alessandro Bastoni, however, agent Tullio Tinti firmly applied the brakes, as reported by Calciomercato.com: “Alessandro is definitely staying at Inter, this is his home.” From Roma, meanwhile, Nerazzurri enquiries have emerged for Manu Koné and Gianluca Mancini.

At Juventus the news of the day concerns the goalkeeper: according to Fanpage, an agreement is in place with Emiliano “Dibu” Martinez, on his way out of Aston Villa. For Alexander Sørloth, Corriere della Sera reports, 25-30 million are needed to finalise the deal with Atlético Madrid. The Bianconeri are also tracking Mattia Liberali: Calciomercato.com takes stock of who wants him, his release clause and how much Milan would be owed from a sale.

Milan are getting down to business for the bench: on Oliver Glasner's table, writes Calciomercato.com, there is a two-year deal with an option, but a technical director must come first, and Diretta reports a stalemate in the talks. Rafael Leao, interviewed by Rtp, meanwhile reiterates his desire to leave: “I would leave Milan happy and satisfied.” Gasperini's Roma are working on several fronts: Calciomercato.com flags interest in Atlético Madrid's Facundo Ruggeri, while Corriere della Sera reports contacts for youngster Alajbegovic. Napoli are probing new avenues: rumours of Federico Chiesa from Liverpool and Real Sociedad's Remiro for the goal, while McTominay is being linked with Tottenham.

Abroad, Real Madrid dominate the scene, described as “unleashed” by Diretta: the Gvardiol idea from Manchester City has surfaced, with Rudiger heading for the exit and Dumfries already closing in from Inter. In La Liga, Isco's move from Real Betis to Sevilla is official, as is Muriqi's from Mallorca to Fenerbahçe. As for big-spending newly promoted sides, Pisa land the double signing of Joao Pedro from Chelsea and Lorenzo Lucca from Nottingham Forest, and also welcome Alessandro Arena from Roma. In Serie A, finally, talk of Francesco Camarda moving from Lecce to Cagliari is gaining traction.
