Sørloth to Juve, Dumfries to Real: June 9 sets the market on fire

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 9 Jun 2026

Juventus seal Alexander Sørloth from Atlético Madrid as Denzel Dumfries leaves Inter for Real Madrid and Mkhitaryan extends his stay. A hectic day also produces the Julian Álvarez bombshell, another wave of Milan departures, and Hojlund confirmed at Napoli.

Juventus close the Vlahovic chapter and open the Sørloth era. Dušan Vlahović is officially heading to Barcelona, while Douglas Luiz — signed from Aston Villa last summer — has been formally confirmed as a Juventus player. The key transfer of the day is Alexander Sørloth: the Norwegian striker's deal with Atlético Madrid is confirmed (Calciomercato.com, Fanpage), with his medical reportedly completed during the Norwegian national team's training camp. On the goalkeeping front, Juventus are intensifying contacts for Emiliano 'Dibu' Martínez of Aston Villa (Sky Sport, Calciomercato.com), and Franck Kessié remains a midfield target.

Inter are rebuilding with purpose. On the way out, Denzel Dumfries' transfer to Real Madrid is confirmed; Henrikh Mkhitaryan, however, signs a contract extension with the club — Calciomercato.com reveals today that the Armenian conducted the renewal without an agent. On the incoming side, Inter are stepping up their pursuit of Giorgio Palestra from Atalanta with an improved bid of €45m (Calciomercato.com), while Lazio have set a price of €5m for Ivan Provedel (Gazzetta dello Sport). Sky Sport reports contacts with Udinese over Oumar Solet and young winger Atta. Out the door: Sebastiano Esposito (Cagliari) and Taye Buchanan (Villarreal).

The day's biggest headline comes from Spain: Real Madrid have set their sights on Julián Álvarez, reportedly ready to invest €150m to prise him from Atlético Madrid. The colchoneros respond with a flat no, invoking a release clause worth half a billion euros (Calciomercato.com). This standoff looks set to dominate the coming weeks and has already raised the temperature of the entire summer window. Atlético must also process the confirmed departure of Sørloth to Juventus.

Milan's clear-out continues. The list of official departures grows longer: Noah Okafor to Leeds, Chukwueze to Fulham, Alex Jimenez to Bournemouth, and Lorenzo Colombo stays at Genoa via a permanent deal. On the bench, uncertainty persists: Oliver Glasner says he is waiting for Milan, but Gerry Cardinale must first settle the Rangnick question (Calciomercato.com). In lighter news, Time magazine has named Cardinale among the 100 most influential figures in world sport. Incoming targets include Carlos Summerville (West Ham) and Adama Traoré.

Napoli complete a productive session. Rasmus Hojlund is official from Manchester United, Miguel Gutiérrez has touched down after leaving Girona, and Joao Gomes from Wolves is in a confirmed state of agreement. Out go Rafa Marín to Villarreal and Natan to Real Betis. Bologna officially unveil Domenico Tedesco as their new head coach; Roma consolidate their window with Mason Greenwood (Manchester United, conf.) and Bryan Zaragoza (Bayern, conf.). Lazio, meanwhile, sell Mandas to Bournemouth, Vecino to Celta Vigo, and Muriqi to Mallorca, embarking on a deep squad overhaul.
