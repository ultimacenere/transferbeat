DAILY RECAP · 25 Aug 2026

Di Gregorio and Savinho confirmed, Pinamonti joins Lazio

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 25 Aug 2026

Tuesday 25 August brought two Premier League confirmations: Michele Di Gregorio moves from Juventus to Bournemouth and Savinho joins Tottenham from Manchester City. In Italy, Lazio wrapped up Andrea Pinamonti, while Rafael Leao's future stayed in the spotlight.

The two done deals of the day both came from the Premier League. According to Calciomercato.com, Michele Di Gregorio is a new Bournemouth player: the goalkeeper leaves Juventus on loan with an option to buy, a move also reported earlier in the day by Gianluca Di Marzio.

Still in England, Calciomercato.com confirms Savinho's switch to Tottenham: the Brazilian winger arrives from Manchester City for 75 million pounds.

In Serie A, Lazio landed their striker. Calciomercato.com reports Andrea Pinamonti's arrival from Sassuolo as done, on a paid loan worth 3 million euros. Among Sassuolo's outgoing moves, Sebastiano Esposito is also on the way out: Calciomercato.com says the deal is close as part of a double loan swap, with Ciervo heading to Cagliari.

Rafael Leao's future remains a talking point. Calciomercato.com rates the Aston Villa route as very complicated for now, with the English club deeming the operation beyond reach; meanwhile, Gianluca Di Marzio reports a meeting at Casa Milan with the Portuguese's representative, also attended by Jorge Mendes, to discuss the number 10's future. For Milan's attacking-midfield slot, Calciomercato.com flags a new name: Alvyn Sanches, a 2003-born player from Young Boys.

Several Italian sides stayed busy. Gianluca Di Marzio reports that Napoli have begun contacts for Guiu, top of their list up front, and that Juventus keep targeting Alexander Sorloth as their centre-forward, with a possible swap on the table. On the Inter front, Di Marzio again notes Sassuolo back in contact over a return for Francesco Acerbi, a defender also drawing interest from Bologna; on the way out, according to Calciomercato.com, Al Jazira have contacted the Nerazzurri for Kristjan Asllani, whom Inter would rather sell permanently.

At Atalanta there is pressure from the Premier League: Calciomercato.com reports that Chelsea, after Palestra, have opened contacts for Honest Ahanor. Atalanta themselves remain in the race for Noa Lang alongside Bologna, with the Dutchman's future, per Calciomercato.com, potentially tied to Rowe's.
