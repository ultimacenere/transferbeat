Vicario to Juventus: deal with Tottenham close

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 17 Aug 2026

Juventus have settled their goalkeeper question and are pushing for Guglielmo Vicario: according to Sportmediaset there is an agreement with the player and a deal with Tottenham is close. Eurosport and Gazzetta dello Sport report that Spurs are open to a loan, while Sky Sport speaks of ongoing contacts to close.

The move stems from Vicario's situation in London. About three weeks ago Goal.com reported that the goalkeeper had slipped to third choice at Tottenham, dropped by De Zerbi: a situation that turned him into a market opportunity for the Italian clubs, Juventus and Napoli first among them.

The first concrete signal towards Turin came in mid-August. On the 14th, Gazzetta dello Sport wrote that Vicario had opened the door to Juventus, explaining why a short-term, «use-and-return» type of operation could also suit the player, eager to be a first-choice again.

The turning point came in the last hours. On Saturday evening Calciomercato.com reported a negotiation under way between Juventus and Tottenham, stressing that the goalkeeper had already said yes to the Bianconeri project. Today Sportmediaset spoke openly of the goalkeeper question being resolved: an agreement with Vicario and an understanding with the English club now close.

On the formula, Eurosport and Gazzetta dello Sport agree: Tottenham are open to a loan. Gazzetta adds that, in parallel, the alternative route leading to Dibu Martínez did not convince, pushing the management to choose the Italian. Sky Sport confirms the picture, reporting ongoing contacts between the two clubs to reach a conclusion.

On the TransferBeat board the status of the deal is «conf»: the agreement with the player is there, and the parties are working to define the final details with Tottenham.

What is missing: the definitive agreement between the clubs on the transfer formula and then the usual final steps, from the medical to the signature, up to the official announcement. Sources do not report precise figures for the deal, which therefore remain to be confirmed.
