Liverpool's first win: an Isak double sinks Ipswich

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 5 Sep 2026

Liverpool grab their first league win: 2-0 at Ipswich, sealed by Alexander Isak. It is the first victory of the Iraola era after two draws.

At Portman Road, Liverpool beat Ipswich Town 2-0 on Premier League matchday three. The game was effectively settled in the first half: the Reds went in 2-0 up at the break, then managed the lead after the interval without conceding.

The star of the night was Alexander Isak. According to Corriere dello Sport, the striker scored both goals of the evening, delivering a win the side had been missing since the start of the season. In the same match, the paper reports, Barcola also made his debut in a Reds shirt.

It is the first win of the Iraola era on the Liverpool bench. In the opening two rounds the Reds had settled for as many draws, including a 2-2 at home to Nottingham: the table now reads five points from three games, with a record of one win and two draws.

Isak's personal tally also puts him top of the Premier League scoring charts: the Swede leads the way with 3 goals, ahead of a chasing group on 2 that includes Saka, Palmer and Haaland. A start that is carrying the team through its tougher moments.

The picture at the top remains crowded: Manchester City, Arsenal and Hull City lead with a perfect 6 points from two games, while Liverpool try to close in with one match more already played.

Now the focus turns to the European week. Liverpool open their Champions League league phase on 9 September, at home to Atletico Madrid; then it is back to the Premier League on 12 September, again in front of their own fans against Fulham. In the background, a Reds rumour: according to Liverpool FC, Suso is said to have announced his retirement from football.
