27 August: Chelsea-Aston Villa swap, Kean and Ricci to Como

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 27 Aug 2026

On Thursday 27 August the Premier League lit up the market with a striker swap between Chelsea and Aston Villa and with Bradley Barcola joining Liverpool. Serie A saw a flurry of official deals, from Como’s double swoop (Kean and Ricci) to Pinamonti signing for Lazio and Ilić for Lecce.

The summer window served up a red-hot day in the Premier League, headlined by a striker swap between Chelsea and Aston Villa. According to Calciomercato.com it is a “here we go” for Emiliano Martínez to Chelsea, with a deal agreed with Aston Villa worth 7.5 million pounds; moving the other way is Nicolas Jackson, whom Aston Villa sign from the Blues for 65 million pounds. Also from Chelsea, Calciomercato.com reports the arrival of Honest Ahanor from Atalanta for 40 million pounds. Completing the English day, Liverpool wrap up Bradley Barcola from PSG in a 100-million-pound operation and Tottenham confirm Omar Marmoush from Manchester City on loan with an obligation to buy.

Serie A saw several official deals. Calciomercato.com announces the “here we go” of Moise Kean to Como, signed from Fiorentina on a paid loan worth 10 million euros, and that of Samuele Ricci, also joining Como from Milan on a paid loan worth 1.5 million euros. It is also done for Giovanni Fabbian to Parma, on loan with an option to buy from Fiorentina. On the signings front, Andrea Pinamonti is officially a new Lazio player (from Sassuolo for 3 million plus bonuses), Ivan Ilić moves to Lecce from Torino on loan with an option, and Daniel Bîrligea joins Frosinone from FCSB on a paid loan worth 1.5 million.

Torino are among the busiest clubs: Calciomercato.com reports the completion of Lucas Perri from Leeds on loan with an option to buy, while on the way out, besides Ilić, the Granata sell Marcus Pedersen to Olympiacos for 8 million euros plus a percentage of any future resale. In La Liga, meanwhile, Dominik Livaković is official at Barcelona: the goalkeeper arrives from Fenerbahçe for 2 million euros.

On the incoming market, Napoli are active. Calciomercato.com flags contact with Newcastle for Nick Woltemade, considered on loan, and reports the basis of a contractual agreement with Marc Guiu, with the Spanish striker keen to play in Italy. Leaving the Azzurri, Avellino have closed for Walid Cheddira in a deal worth 2.5 million euros plus bonuses tied to goals and promotion.

Among the other Italian negotiations, Juventus are pushing for Franck Kessié: Calciomercato.com explains the deal no longer depends on outgoings. Gianluca Di Marzio also reports as official the arrival in Turin of Kamil Grabara from Wolfsburg, on loan worth 0.5 million plus bonuses. Inter arrange a midfield departure with Kristjan Asllani heading to Al Jazira on loan worth 2 million with an obligation to buy, per Di Marzio, while Hellas Verona have closed for Gabriele Zappa from Cagliari, with an obligation to buy in the event of promotion.

Rafael Leão’s future remains up in the air. Calciomercato.com reports that Galatasaray are awaiting the Portuguese’s decision after reaching an agreement with Milan yesterday for 45 million euros. The day was also marked by the group-stage draw of the 2026/27 Champions League.
