LUNCH BREAK · 3 Jul 2026

Provedel to Inter, City break the bank for Anderson, Milan on Gila

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 3 Jul 2026

The midday main course is served: Inter close with Lazio for Provedel (3 million), while in the Premier League Manchester City break the bank for Elliot Anderson, 135 million to Nottingham Forest and a new record for a British player. Around the table Milan barge in hard for Gila, Juventus keep Bayern at bay over Bremer, and Tottenham prepare a Tonali-Leão double.

Inter bring the first course to the table: Calciomercato.com reports the «deal between Inter and Lazio for Provedel is done», a 3 million euro operation that Corriere dello Sport confirms as an agreement between the clubs. Ivan Provedel strengthens the Nerazzurri goal, but the menu does not end there: according to Sky Sport, Inter are pushing hard for Union Saint-Gilloise's Khalaili — 25 million the asking price and an open duel with Napoli — while Chalobah remains a warm name for the defence.

The heavyweight course, as tradition dictates, speaks English. Manchester City break the bank for Elliot Anderson: 135 million paid to Nottingham Forest, a figure that — Gianluca Di Marzio and eurosport.it report — makes him the most expensive British footballer ever. De Zerbi's Tottenham are no less busy: according to Calciomercato.com they have agreed a deal for Sandro Tonali on the basis of over 100 million (116 per eurosport.it), set to become the most expensive Italian in history, and have already opened first contacts with Milan for Rafael Leão.

And Milan are precisely the table where the most is cooking. Coming in, Calciomercato.com flags a «strong move» for Mario Gila — another Lazio gem, after Provedel's departure — with contacts already opened with his agent and the leapfrogging of Napoli confirmed by Sky Sport and eurosport.it. On the way out, the future of Leão is on the menu: the Rossoneri value him at 60-70 million and, the same source leaks, after the World Cup there will be a contact with the Portuguese to «decide together» what comes next, with Tottenham already waiting in the wings.

In the capital, Roma set two places. Coming in, they have asked Chelsea for information on Alejandro Garnacho (Calciomercato.com), while to complete the assault on Tresoldi they must first free up Dovbyk: Genoa have moved on the striker and — Sky Sport reports — are attempting a loan. Staying in the international kitchen, for Mason Greenwood an official bid has arrived from Atlético Madrid to Olympique Marseille, with the Giallorossi yet to leave the table. Juventus, for their part, are keeping Bayern Munich's exploratory sounding for Bremer at bay and closing in on the return of Muharemovic from Sassuolo, ready to sign until 2031.

Among the side dishes served hot: Luis Milla is officially a Como player (from Getafe), Carboni moves permanently from Inter to Parma, Lucas Beltrán flies out on loan from Fiorentina to River Plate, and Genoa — besides sounding out Aubameyang — have landed David Puczka from Juventus for 6 million. The coffee, as always, is served by the World Cup: Spain swept Austria aside 3-0 (an Oyarzabal brace and a Porro strike), while Portugal edged Croatia 2-1 with a Cristiano Ronaldo penalty and a Gonçalo Ramos flash set up by none other than Leão. And speaking of CR7: according to Calciomercato.com he will leave the national team after this World Cup. Enjoy your meal — the transfer table stays set.
