Rodri to Barcelona: full agreement reached with City

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 17 Aug 2026

Barcelona have reached a full agreement with Manchester City for Rodri, with Calciomercato.com declaring "here we go" for the Spanish midfielder. According to Calciomercato the deal is worth over 75 million euros, while La Gazzetta dello Sport reports the agreement is done and that City have already lined up a replacement.

The talks heated up over recent days. Three days ago Tuttosport described a Manchester City that "wouldn't budge", with a Barcelona offer rejected and a counter ready; shortly after, Sportal.it called the operation "pure madness", putting its total value at 210 million euros.

In the following hours the signals grew more concrete. Eurosport placed Rodri "one step" from the Catalans, while Calcio.com reported a fresh Barcelona push, with just ten million separating the club from Manchester City's demands.

The turning point came yesterday. Calciomercato ran the headline "it's done", reporting a full agreement with Manchester City and a deal worth over 75 million euros; that evening Calciomercato.com launched its "here we go", confirming the agreement for the Spanish midfielder.

La Gazzetta dello Sport reinforced the picture, stating the agreement is in place and that City have already identified a replacement. On the TransferBeat board the status of the deal is now "done".

After the "here we go", only the usual formal steps remain, starting with the official announcement from both clubs. On the figures the sources are not fully aligned — ranging from the "over 75 million" cited by Calciomercato to the 210 million total previously mentioned by Sportal.it — so the definitive financial details are still to be confirmed.
