DAILY RECAP · 8 Jun 2026

Sørloth to Juve confirmed, Milan clears out five, Napoli completes treble

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 8 Jun 2026

Juventus lock down Alexander Sørloth from Atlético Madrid alongside Senesi and Douglas Luiz, while Napoli celebrate Hojlund, Joao Gomes and Gutierrez in one sweep. Milan steal the headlines with five departures confirmed in a single day and two head-coach candidates — Glasner and Rangnick — reportedly ready to sign.

Juventus have their striker. Calciomercato.com reports that the deal for Alexander Sørloth from Atlético Madrid is effectively done: Spalletti pushed hard for the Norwegian, convinced he fits the profile for his front three. Marcos Senesi from Bournemouth joins in defence, with Douglas Luiz from Aston Villa already in the fold in midfield. On the exit side, Vlahovic continues to attract Barcelona, while Cambiaso has Chelsea interested. Bernardo Silva and Brahim Díaz — the latter leaving Milan — are names circling as further additions.

Napoli are building something serious. Rasmus Hojlund arrives from Manchester United, Miguel Gutierrez seals a move from Girona, and Joao Gomes from Wolverhampton is close to completion. Three signings that give Conte a proper striker, a dynamic full-back and added steel in midfield. Natan heads to Real Betis. Vlahovic's name is also doing the rounds, with Chelsea as a potential middle-man.

Milan's fire sale is now official. Five departures land in one day: Yunus Musah to Atalanta, Samuel Chukwueze to Fulham, Noah Okafor to Leeds United, Lorenzo Colombo to Genoa, Patrick Cutrone to Wolverhampton. Pervis Estupiñán from Brighton comes in to cover the left flank. On the bench, Calciomercato.com confirms both Oliver Glasner and Ralf Rangnick are ready to take charge — Rangnick could bring David Alaba on a free — while David Planes, who worked at Espanyol and Barcelona, emerges as a potential sporting director option.

Inter confirm Dumfries departure and keep Calhanoglu. The Denzel Dumfries-to-Real Madrid operation is confirmed, ending the Dutch full-back's stint in Milan. Filippo Stankovic returns to the club and Sebastiano Esposito joins Cagliari. In terms of arrivals, Matteo Palestra from Atalanta remains the primary target, but the day's most intriguing snippet — per Calciomercato.com — is Curtis Jones asking Federico Chiesa what life in Italy is like, hinting at genuine interest in a Serie A move. Hakan Calhanoglu stays put: Inter shut down Turkish enquiries with a firm no.

Roma are buying, Lazio are selling. Roma welcome Evan Ferguson from Brighton, Sandy Malen from Aston Villa and Oscar Zaragoza from Bayern Munich. Enzo Le Fée departs to Sunderland. Greenwood and Igor Paixão from Marseille remain in the pipeline. Lazio move in the opposite direction: Manu Pedraza arrives from Villarreal but Nikola Mandas leaves for Bournemouth and Matías Vecino ends his time at the club, joining Celta Vigo.

Manchester United are reshaping their squad fast. Hojlund is already in Naples, Antony has signed for Real Betis, while Bryan Mbeumo from Brentford and Matheus Cunha from Wolverhampton come in. The headline name is Sandro Tonali: United are tracking the Newcastle midfielder seriously, per Calciomercato.com. Real Betis land a double with Antony and Natan. Atalanta pick up Musah from Milan and are keeping a close eye on De Ketelaere being used as a centre-forward by Belgium boss Garcia.
