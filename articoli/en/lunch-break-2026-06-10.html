Lunch Break: Inter close in on Solet, Juve haggle over Dibu Martinez

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 10 Jun 2026

Inter are accelerating with Udinese for Oumar Solet while keeping the Atta track warm, and Juventus are negotiating a discount with Aston Villa for Emiliano Martinez. Lunch in Madrid is spicy too: Julián Álvarez stirs both sides of the city, with Real also sounding out Calafiori.

The main course is served in Milan. According to Calciomercato.com, Chivu's Inter are "flexing their muscles": Solet is getting closer in a €25m deal with Udinese, and Arthur Atta's name also came up in the summit with the Friulani. Sky Sport confirms the trend: Inter are ever closer to the French defender, with only a small gap left to close. In the background, a Gianluca Mancini idea from Roma has emerged, while Dumfries' exit towards Real Madrid remains confirmed.

At Juventus the file of the day is between the posts: per Calciomercato.com, the Dibu Martinez deal "can now be done", with Aston Villa asking for €15m and the Bianconeri working on a discount. Up front, Sorloth from Atlético Madrid remains the frontrunner, now at the confirmed stage. Midfield chapter: Calciomercato.com also describes Teun Koopmeiners as "in the balance" in Turin — fun fact: his brother Peer is admired by Bologna — and since the morning the Lobotka-from-Napoli suggestion has been doing the rounds.

Napoli, between deals and flag-bearers. Agent Giuffredi, on Di Lorenzo, is blunt: "He wants to stay for life, we'll see if the club feels the same" (source: Calciomercato.com). In goal, with Meret's future still unwritten, Remiro of Real Sociedad has been circulating since this morning; on the flank, Sevilla's Juanlu remains a live target.

Roma are working several tables: Calciomercato.com's live blog has them on Atlético Madrid's Facundo Ruggeri, while the Mathys Tel objective from Tottenham gains traction. On the way out, beyond the Mancini-Inter idea, watch the foreign pressing on Soulé. On the Milan side, words rather than deeds: Gimenez pledges Rossoneri love — "I've supported Milan since I was a kid, the fans trust me" — just as his name circulates in a Lazio key, and Evani blesses a possible Rangnick arrival: "He has clear ideas".

An international finish. In Madrid the case of the day is still Julián Álvarez, a declared Real target that rattles Atlético; Los Blancos are also sounding out Arsenal's Calafiori, while the Colchoneros return the favour to their Premier League rivals by negotiating for Chelsea's Cucurella. In Andalusia, meanwhile, one deal is already on ice: Isco leaves Betis and crosses the city — it's done with Sevilla.
