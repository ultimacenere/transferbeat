Summerville: Roma ready with €45m, only the yes is missing

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 16 Jul 2026

Roma have a €45m offer ready for Crysencio Summerville and are waiting for the final green light to close the deal with West Ham. Sky Sport reports a huge offer and Calciomercato.com a move that only needs the Dutchman's definitive OK, while Aston Villa remain on alert in the background.

Roma have decided to go all in for Crysencio Summerville. On Wednesday evening Calciomercato.com broke the news: the Giallorossi have a €45m offer ready for West Ham's Dutch winger, with «only the player's definitive OK» still missing at that point. The same figure was echoed by Pagine Romaniste, which spoke of a «total sprint» with €45m ready while awaiting the Dutchman's yes.

The negotiation heated up over the last few hours. Sky Sport had reported the meeting between Roma and Summerville's agents; once the talks ended, Pagine Romaniste said the club was waiting for West Ham's response. Il Messaggero then flagged «steps forward», with the ball now in the player's court.

Then came the acceleration. Sky Sport described a «huge offer» ready for West Ham, while Corriere della Sera portrayed a Roma «ready to do whatever it takes» for the Dutchman. The valuation hovers around the €45m cited by several sources.

Not everything, however, is settled yet. Corriere dello Sport, while placing Moreira close to Roma, stresses that for Summerville «there is still no agreement, but they are working on it». And Aston Villa remain on watch too: LaRoma24 is expecting «the player's decision today», which will be decisive in unlocking the deal.

Summerville is the first choice for the Roma attack, in a window where the club is also working on Moreira — La Gazzetta dello Sport reports the player's yes and a €10m gap with Strasbourg — and keeps alive its interest in Real Madrid's Endrick. A sale could help fund the push: for Koné, tracked by Manchester United and Chelsea, Il Riformista indicates a €60m asking price.
