Palestra to Inter closing in: agent in Milan to seal it

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 22 Jun 2026

The Palestra-Inter deal has entered its decisive phase: Sky Sport calls it «close to completion» with Atalanta, while Calciomercato.com reports that the player's agent is expected in Milan «to close» the deal and settle the figures. After the verbal agreement reached in mid-June, the Nerazzurri are now only waiting for the signature, with Palestra having always preferred Inter to the lure of the Premier League.

The last few hours have pushed the negotiation towards the finish line. Sky Sport describes Palestra-Inter as a deal «close to completion with Atalanta» and, today, repeats that the player is «closer and closer»; SNAI sportnews goes further with a «done for Palestra», asking only «when the signing can arrive». Gianluca Di Marzio is on the same page, opening his market day with the Nerazzurri target's name.

The most concrete signal comes from the diary. Calciomercato.com reports that «Palestra's agent is expected in Milan to close» the deal and to settle its figures; Corriere della Sera openly talks of «Palestra's days» at Inter, while Quotidiano Sportivo sees the Nerazzurri «one step away». In recent days, from Bergamo, the Atalanta camp itself was already treating the sale as «only a matter of time».

The thread runs back to mid-June. Eurosport had revealed the «verbal agreement» between the two clubs for a deal «worth almost €50 million», with the talks reduced «to the bonuses alone»; La Gazzetta dello Sport and Calciomercato.com had reported a «positive» meeting with Atalanta and an improved Inter bid, later noted live by Virgilio Sport as well.

The real turning point, even before the price, was the player's will. For weeks the move faced top-level competition: La Gazzetta dello Sport reported «Newcastle and Manchester City» calling and Sky Sport spoke of «two Premier League giants» on Palestra, with Eurosport pointing to Frattesi as the piece able to «unlock» the deal. The player's answer, though, never changed: Inter as the only choice.

The final chapter still has to be written. The detailed figures of the operation and of the wages have not yet emerged in the news reported so far, and above all the official confirmation is missing: the signature. Once the agent has settled the last details in Milan and Inter and Atalanta have formalised the agreement, Palestra will be able to become an Inter player in every respect.
