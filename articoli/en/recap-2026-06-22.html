Vicario to Inter, Dibu to Juve: a day for goalkeepers

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 22 Jun 2026

A day of goalkeepers on the market: Inter strike an agreement with Tottenham for Vicario and Juventus close in on Aston Villa's Emiliano «Dibu» Martínez, while in La Liga Barcelona land Nico Williams. In Serie A, Milan's clear-out continues — out go Musah, Chukwueze, Gabbia and Suso — as Napoli and Roma seal the signings of Lucca and Malen, all against the backdrop of the World Cup in the United States.

The deal of the day at Inter carries Vicario's name: the Nerazzurri have reached an agreement with Tottenham for the goalkeeper, ready to take over between the posts. The club also keep alive their interest in Ivan Provedel, on his way out of Lazio, and in Udinese's Oumar Solet for the defence, while in midfield the trail to Liverpool's Curtis Jones is still warm.

Juventus land their reinforcement in goal with an agreement for Emiliano «Dibu» Martínez, arriving from Aston Villa. Up front the Bianconeri keep courting Atlético Madrid's Aleksandar Sørloth, also a Napoli target, and keep the Kolo Muani trail alive as the Frenchman heads out of Tottenham; on the way out, Andrea Cambiaso is being tracked by Chelsea. Shoring up the defence is Gleison Bremer, who — Calciomercato.com reports — has no intention of leaving Turin this summer.

On to Milan, deep into a squad clear-out. The Rossoneri wave goodbye to Yunus Musah, off to Atalanta, Samuel Chukwueze, flying to Fulham, and Matteo Gabbia, now at Villarreal; Suso also departs, his deal made permanent by Sevilla, along with young Francesco Camarda, loaned on to Lecce. Moves that lighten both squad and wage bill, while on the left flank interest remains alive in Brighton's Pervis Estupiñán.

At Roma, in comes Donyell Malen as the new attacking winger, while talks continue with Bayern Munich over Bryan Zaragoza. On the way out, Tommaso Baldanzi returns to Genoa, Angeliño has caught the eye of Betis, and for Nicolò Zaniolo there is interest from Lazio, ready — according to Calciomercato.com — to bring him back to the capital. On the incoming front, Marseille's Mason Greenwood remains a hot name.

Napoli pull off the signing of Lorenzo Lucca, a striker taken from Nottingham Forest, and close in on Mario Gila, arriving from Lazio for the back line. Elsewhere in Serie A the other clubs are busy too: Bologna start afresh under new coach Domenico Tedesco, Cagliari — Calciomercato.com reports — are targeting Pisa's Michael Aebischer, valued at five million, and Venezia have set their sights on several Hellas Verona players, from Lorenzo Montipò to Domagoj Bradarić and Armel Bella-Kotchap.

Abroad, Barcelona complete a double swoop on the wings: after Nico Williams from Athletic Club, the Blaugrana also have an agreement for Newcastle's Anthony Gordon. In the Premier League, Manchester United welcome Bryan Mbeumo from Brentford, while Manchester City weigh selling Bernardo Silva to Real Madrid. All of this against the backdrop of the World Cup in the United States, where Lionel Messi has become the competition's all-time top scorer in Argentina's 1-0 win over Austria (Calciomercato.com).
