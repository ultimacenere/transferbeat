Dumfries joins Real Madrid for €20m as Milan land Gonçalo Ramos

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 5 Jul 2026

Real Madrid have made Denzel Dumfries official, signing the Dutchman from Inter for €20m in the biggest move of a busy Sunday. In Serie A, Milan complete their attack with Gonçalo Ramos and push for Mateta and Gila, Juventus close in on goalkeeper Dibu Martínez, and in the Premier League Manchester United confirm Bryan Mbeumo.

The deal of the day comes from Spain: Denzel Dumfries is a new Real Madrid player, with the club paying Inter €20m for the Dutch full-back, as reported by Calciomercato.com. Inter, who lose a first-choice wide man, have meanwhile secured goalkeeper Ivan Provedel from Lazio.

In Milan it is the Rossoneri setting the pace. Up front the arrival of Gonçalo Ramos from Paris Saint-Germain is done, while deals for Jean-Philippe Mateta from Crystal Palace and defender Mario Gila from Lazio move towards completion. On the way out, Samuel Chukwueze is heading to Fulham and Noah Okafor to Leeds.

The rest of Serie A is busy too. Atalanta land Gianluca Gaetano from Cagliari, Genoa welcome Emanuele Baldanzi from Roma and Como wrap up the arrival of Luis Milla from Getafe. Juventus are close to goalkeeper Emiliano 'Dibu' Martínez of Aston Villa, while Lazio sell Cristian Mandas to Bournemouth.

In the Premier League, Manchester United have confirmed Bryan Mbeumo from Brentford, while Tottenham press for Sandro Tonali of Newcastle. Bournemouth are active as well, adding defender Huijsen from Real Madrid alongside Mandas, and Sunderland welcome Jobe Bellingham from Borussia Dortmund.

There are big departures and negotiations too. Paulo Dybala is close to joining Boca Juniors, set to leave Roma, while Fiorentina are trying to bring Radu Drăgușin back to Italy: according to Calciomercato.com the Viola would pay Tottenham a total of €16.5m. In the background remain talk of Scott McTominay at Napoli and the future of Davide Frattesi at Inter.

In Spain, beyond the Dumfries coup, Real Madrid keep trimming their squad after an already busy incoming market. With dozens of moves across Serie A, La Liga and the Premier League packed into a few hours, the summer window springs to life, even if several of the open threads are still to be made official.
