DAILY RECAP · 5 Sep 2026

Inter perfect, Fiorentina in deep crisis

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 5 Sep 2026

Inter beat Napoli 3-2 to stay perfect in Serie A, while Fiorentina lost 1-2 to Torino and remain bottom on zero points. A full day too in the Premier League, Bundesliga and Ligue 1, with Roma-Atalanta and more matches tonight.

In Serie A, Fiorentina were pegged back and beaten 1-2 at home by Torino in the afternoon, while at 18:00 Inter edged Napoli 3-2 in the San Siro big match. In the Premier League most of matchday three was played: Manchester City beat Coventry 1-0, Tottenham took their first point in a 0-0 at Nottingham, while Newcastle-Bournemouth (2-2), Brighton-Leeds (1-1), Brentford-Sunderland (1-1) and Fulham-Crystal Palace (2-3) completed the picture. In the Bundesliga, Leverkusen thrashed Union Berlin 4-0, Dortmund won 3-2 at Hoffenheim, Werder Bremen beat Leipzig 3-1, Elversberg won 4-3 at Gladbach and Freiburg won 1-0 at Paderborn. In Ligue 1, Lorient sprang a 0-1 surprise at Lens.

In the table, Inter stay on maximum points, top with 9 after three wins, ahead of Como (7). Fiorentina are bottom and still on zero after three defeats, with the league's worst goal difference, while Napoli slip to 3 points. In the Premier League, Manchester City lead with 9 points, in Ligue 1 Monaco top the table on maximum points, and in the Bundesliga, pending the late game, Freiburg, Dortmund and Elversberg share the lead on 6. LaLiga does not play this round: Barcelona, Real Madrid and Betis remain level on 9 points, with the next round from 16 September.

The story of the day is Fiorentina's crisis: a third straight defeat for Grosso's side, jeered by the Franchi crowd, with former Viola man Mandragora the match-winner who did not celebrate and apologised (Sky Sport, Corriere dello Sport). Grosso ruled out resigning, «I'm not resigning, that's for those above me to decide» (ANSA Calcio), but according to Goal.com the club is weighing his position. At Inter, after beating Napoli, Marotta widened the title race: «Roma, Juve... and Como are in it too» (ANSA Calcio). On the injury front, Udinese's Arizala is sidelined (Il Friuli).

Attention is already on tomorrow's big match, Juventus-Milan: Spalletti praised his rivals («Amorim has had a strong Milan built for him», ANSA Calcio), while Amorim rated his own transfer window — «I'd give Milan a 10» — defending Tomori's reintegration (Corriere dello Sport).

Several matches were still to finish in the evening: at 20:45 Roma host Atalanta at the Olimpico, with Dybala recovered and in the squad (fantacalcio.it), and in Ligue 1 Le Havre-Brest and Nice-Le Mans were on; at the time of the data update, Hull City-Aston Villa in the Premier League and Schalke-Bayern in the Bundesliga were still in progress.

Tomorrow the focus is mainly on Serie A: at 15:00 Parma-Monza and Frosinone-Venezia, at 18:00 Bologna-Sassuolo and at 20:45 the big match Juventus-Milan. The Premier League closes matchday three with Everton-Manchester United and Arsenal-Chelsea, the Bundesliga with Hamburg-Mainz and Frankfurt-Augsburg, and Ligue 1 with Troyes-Strasbourg, Angers-Rennes and Marseille-Paris FC.
