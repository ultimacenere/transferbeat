Goncalo Ramos to Milan, it's done: EUR 70m to PSG, a club-record signing

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 26 Jun 2026

Transfer breaking news. Milan land a front-page coup: Goncalo Ramos arrives from PSG for around EUR 70m (EUR 65m fixed plus EUR 5m in bonuses). It is the most expensive signing in Milan's history, beating Leao's record. Medical already done and the player's yes: it's done.

A record deal. As reported by The Athletic and confirmed by Sky Sport and Fabrizio Romano, Milan have closed for Goncalo Ramos on the basis of EUR 65m fixed plus EUR 5m in bonuses: an outlay of around EUR 70m that rewrites the club's history, surpassing the EUR 50m spent years ago on Rafael Leao from Lille.

A Portuguese centre-forward born in 2001, Ramos has been at PSG since the summer of 2023 and lifted back-to-back Champions League titles with the Parisians, in 2025 and 2026. A big-stage profile, physical and technical at once, whom Milan identified as their first choice up front for 2026-27.

The operation was handled in great secrecy: the medical, it is said, was already carried out in Florida a couple of weeks ago, when talks between Milan and PSG entered the decisive phase. The player gave his green light some time ago, fully embracing the Rossoneri project.

A signing that carries weight, and not only for the figures: Milan state their ambitions by putting a European champion in attack. The signatures and the announcement remain, but the substance is already written: Goncalo Ramos is a new Milan player.
