LUNCH BREAK · 6 Jun 2026

Stanković officially signs for Inter, Juventus close in on Sørloth

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 6 Jun 2026

Saturday morning delivers one confirmed deal and an accelerating pursuit: Aleksandar Stanković is officially a new Inter player, while Juventus inch closer to Sørloth — the striker has said yes and Atlético Madrid have opened the door.

Inter Milan confirmed the signing of Aleksandar Stanković on Saturday morning, the midfielder officially joining the Nerazzurri with an announcement on the club’s official website. In the background, president Marotta is keeping the Gabriel Palestra file warm: “We like him, but…” he said — a phrase that leaves every door open. Newcastle and Manchester City have come knocking, but Palmeiras would prefer to sell exclusively to Inter (Gazzetta dello Sport).

Juventus are the day’s most active club. The headline story is Alexander Sørloth: the Atlético Madrid striker has given his personal yes to the Bianconeri, and Calciomercato.com and Eurosport report that Nico González could be included as part of the deal. The dossiers don’t end there: Sky Sport and Di Marzio confirm the push for Brahim Díaz, while Gazzetta reports contact with Mike Maignan — whose future at Milan has clouded following the club’s boardroom reset. Federico Chiesa added a significant hint from Liverpool: “Deep down I want to return to Italy. Juventus are in my heart” (Calciomercato.com).

Saturday also brings managerial reshuffles. Fiorentina officially parted ways with Paolo Vanoli and are closing in on Fabio Grosso, who will sign a two-year deal according to Di Marzio and Sky Sport. Grosso leaves Sassuolo, where Alberto Aquilani is now the clear frontrunner to take over, having edged out Torino among other suitors (Di Marzio).

Allegri is focused on keeping Kevin De Bruyne at Napoli; Gazzetta casts him as the club’s future “Modrić figure,” with De Laurentiis determined not to let him go. In Rome, Mason Greenwood’s future is under pressure: agent Safi has publicly declared he has an agreement in place for a Fenerbahçe move (Calciomercato.com). And a name few saw coming: Calciomercato.com reports Palermo have made a contract offer to Paulo Dybala — a reply is expected soon.
