Transfers July 8: Provedel joins Inter, Gaetano to Atalanta, Juve push

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 9 Jul 2026

Inter have officially signed goalkeeper Ivan Provedel, while Atalanta have wrapped up the move for Gianluca Gaetano, who is due for his medical on Thursday. Juventus are living their busiest day: a new bid to PSG for Kolo Muani and first contacts with Aston Villa for Dibu Martinez.

The signing of the day comes at Inter: as reported by Sky Sport, the Nerazzurri have officially completed the arrival of Ivan Provedel, with the fee and contract details already settled. The goalkeeper leaves Lazio, who continue to reshape their squad after the imminent departure of Mario Gila, bound for Milan.

In Serie A, Atalanta have finalised the signing of Gianluca Gaetano: according to Sky Sport it is all done, with the medical scheduled for Thursday. The deal with Cagliari, SOS Fanta reports, closes at around 12 million euros plus bonuses. Also in Serie A, Udinese have officially signed Unai Gomez from Athletic Bilbao, while Torino have tied down Duvan Zapata with a contract renewal (Calciomercato.com).

The busiest day belongs to Juventus. Up front, as Sky Sport reveals, the Bianconeri are preparing a new offer to Paris Saint-Germain for Randal Kolo Muani, with Alexander Sorloth and Pellegrino in the background. In parallel, the club have opened first contacts with Aston Villa for goalkeeper Emiliano 'Dibu' Martinez (Sky Sport, ANSA) and met Udinese for defender Oumar Solet, as confirmed by Calciomercato.com. In the background remains the situation of Dusan Vlahovic, who has asked for a meeting with the club (Il Riformista).

Roma's market is lively too. Their attacking priority remains Mason Greenwood, but the deal is getting complicated: according to Sky Sport, Fenerbahce are trying to get ahead of the Giallorossi, while ANSA confirms the difficulties. On the way out, Artem Dovbyk's move to Genoa is proving tough, as Forzaroma.info reports.

Fiorentina remain on alert for Inao Oulai: contacts with his entourage have never stopped, explains Gianluca Di Marzio, with the Viola also keeping an eye on Real Madrid youngster Victor Valdepenas. On the outgoing front, Ismael Bennacer is close to terminating his contract with Milan, with Qatar as his destination (Calciomercato.com).

Abroad, the storylines involving Italian clubs stay hot. Real Madrid have set their sights on Petar Sucic: for Inter, Calciomercato.com reports, the midfielder is worth 60 million euros. In the Premier League, again according to Calciomercato.com, Manchester United and Chelsea have reached a 50 million pound agreement for a young Brazilian talent.
