LUNCH BREAK · 2 Sep 2026

The morning after: Ausilio quits Inter, Camara-Chelsea collapses

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 2 Sep 2026

The summer window has shut its doors, but the morning-after lunch break is anything but quiet. Between boardroom shocks, heavy injuries and the final wave of official moves, here is what is simmering at midday.

In Milan the twist is not on the pitch but in the boardroom: according to Calciomercato.com, Piero Ausilio is leaving Inter after 28 years, a farewell that closes an era for the Nerazzurri. Inter also take a blow on the field, with Marcus Thuram undergoing knee surgery and expected out for at least four months (Calciomercato.com).

The treatment room claims victims at Lazio too, where Nicolo Rovella has stopped with a soleus strain that will keep him out for around forty days (Calciomercato.com). On the Biancocelesti's outgoing front, meanwhile, Ratkov's loan move to Levante is official, while Alessio Romagnoli is very close to Al Sadd with a full 'here we go' (Calciomercato.com).

There is a tail of official moves elsewhere in Serie A too: Fiorentina let M'Bala Nzola join Cagliari, Napoli send Jens Cajuste on loan to Malaga, Como ship Emil Audero to Rayo Vallecano and Monza close for Saba Goglichidze on loan from Udinese (Calciomercato.com). Loan deals that tidy up the squads now that the window is shut.

In the Premier League, Manchester City let Jack Grealish leave, official at Everton on loan, while Arsenal send Ethan Nwaneri to Borussia Dortmund on a straight loan (Calciomercato.com). In La Liga, Atletico Madrid's clear-out continues, with Jose Maria Gimenez to Deportivo La Coruna and Thomas Lemar to Elche both on loan; Deportivo also welcome Marc Casado, on loan from Barcelona (Calciomercato.com).

The loudest twist, though, comes from London: Monaco have cancelled their agreement with Chelsea for Lamine Camara, with the Blues furious after the previous day's 'here we go' on the basis of 55 million (Calciomercato.com). With the market now closed, the lunch break leaves fans with one last dream slipping off the plate.
