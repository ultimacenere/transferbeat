Lunch break: Baldanzi to Genoa, Gaetano to Atalanta, Khalaili wobbles

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 12 Jul 2026

The lunch menu is almost entirely Italian: Genoa have made Baldanzi's move from Roma official, Atalanta have wrapped up Gaetano and Sevilla have triggered Suso's buy-back, cashing Milan in. But the mouthful stuck in the throat is Inter's, because Khalaili's medical came back unfit and CONI has asked for further checks: the Nerazzurri are now looking around.

In Serie A the main course is a string of official stamps. Genoa have announced Emanuele Baldanzi: his exit from Roma is official, complete with structure and club statement according to Retesport and Corriere dello Sport. At Milan, meanwhile, there's a hefty entry in the ledger: Sevilla have triggered their buy-back for Suso and the Rossoneri cash in, as Sky Sport confirms. And it's Gianluca Gaetano time for Atalanta: 'it's done, medicals on Thursday,' Sky reports.

The parade of dishes continues between Florence and the provinces. Fiorentina have all but landed Momodou Atta from Udinese, with Gianluca Di Marzio reporting the medical is already booked. Udinese themselves have made Unai Gomez official from Athletic Bilbao (certified by lottomatica.sport), Como have announced Luis Milla from Getafe, and Cagliari have waved goodbye to Marley Luvumbo, back at Mallorca according to Cagliaripad. To finish, Verona's triple unveiling — Leali, Korac and Cerbone — with Marras flying to Pisa, as Sky Sport sums up.

Not everything on the menu, though, is ready to serve. Inter's move for Youssef Khalaili has soured badly: the medical did not clear him and CONI has asked for further checks, as Tuttosport and Sky Sport report. The upshot, per eurosport.it, is that the deal is at risk and the Nerazzurri have already started looking around: a mouthful that, for now, stays stuck in the throat.

In the Premier League it's records all round. Manchester City have made Matheus Anderson official, who according to eurosport.it becomes the most expensive British player of all time; Liverpool have wrapped up 20-year-old defender Jaquet for 'monster money,' headlines Tuttosport. Meanwhile the figures of Sandro Tonali's move from Newcastle to De Zerbi's Tottenham are emerging: 116 million, Sky Sport reports. Stalled, instead, is the Ederson trail: the deal between Manchester United and Atalanta has collapsed, warns Sky.

We close with the Spanish tray and the afternoon's negotiations. In LaLiga, Atlético Madrid have locked down Morten Hjulmand — 'no Milan,' Sky Sport stresses. Big names stay on the hob: Tottenham have knocked for Milan's Rafael Leao, with Barça in the background (Sportmediaset); Fiorentina are pushing for Real Madrid's Valdepeñas (Di Marzio); Inter are pressing for Jones but Liverpool are holding firm and setting the price (La Gazzetta dello Sport); and Arsenal have an agreement with Roma's Koné (Sportmediaset). Coffee, as always, we'll have tomorrow.
