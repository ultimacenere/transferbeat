July 9 transfers: Khalaili to Inter, Atta to Fiorentina, Hjulmand

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 9 Jul 2026

Inter have wrapped up Anan Khalaili, signed from Union Saint-Gilloise for 25 million euros as the heir to Dumfries, while Fiorentina pull off Paratici's swoop for Arthur Atta from Udinese. In La Liga, Atletico Madrid reach an agreement with Sporting Lisbon for Morten Hjulmand; in the Premier League, Newcastle push for Freiburg's Johan Manzambi.

The deal of the day belongs to Inter. As announced by Calciomercato.com with its trademark 'here we go', the Nerazzurri have reached a full agreement with Union Saint-Gilloise for Anan Khalaili in an operation worth around 25 million euros. The Israeli winger takes over from Dumfries and joins goalkeeper Provedel, made official in recent days, in an incoming market that confirms the drive of Marotta's club.

Fiorentina also landed a surprise signing, closing for Arthur Atta. The deal with Udinese, as reported by Calciomercato.com and Gianluca Di Marzio, was unlocked overnight: the Viola will pay a fee in excess of 20 million euros including bonuses, with a percentage on any future resale. Corriere dello Sport describes a swoop by Paratici, with the midfielder due immediately for his medical.

Other Serie A clubs are moving too. Torino have made Gaetano Oristanio official, arriving from Venezia on loan with an option to buy, while La Gazzetta dello Sport reports an agreement between Udinese and Davis until 2031. At Atalanta, the deal for Gianluca Gaetano is progressing: as confirmed by Sky Sport, the playmaker has completed his medical after reaching terms with Cagliari.

From La Liga comes Atletico Madrid's coup. Again Calciomercato.com delivers another 'here we go': the Colchoneros have closed with Sporting Lisbon for Danish midfielder Morten Hjulmand in a 40 million euro deal. A significant reinforcement for the Colchoneros' midfield.

The Premier League is lively as well. Newcastle, Calciomercato.com reports, are closing in on Freiburg's Johan Manzambi in a deal worth 60 million euros plus bonuses. The final details remain to be settled between Manchester United and Chelsea for Andrey Santos: according to Calciomercato, the two clubs have reached a 50 million pound agreement for the Brazilian midfielder.

On the outgoing front, Calciomercato.com reports that Napoli have opened talks with Schalke 04 for Lindstrom and with Frosinone for Hasa, while Como continue to track young Benfica forward Franjo Ivanovic. At Roma, Greenwood slips away: according to LaRoma24 he has signed with Fenerbahce without the Giallorossi tabling an offer to Marseille. Finally, on the Milan front, Corriere dello Sport reports that Modric's renewal for another year in the red-and-black is drawing closer.
