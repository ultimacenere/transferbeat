Official: Grealish joins Everton on loan from Manchester City

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 2 Sep 2026

Jack Grealish is moving to Everton on loan from Manchester City: the deal is official, as reported by Calciomercato.com. The England forward thus stays in the Premier League after his years with the Citizens.

The news broke on the morning of Wednesday 2 September, in the closing hours of the transfer window: according to Calciomercato.com, Jack Grealish is a new Everton player, joining on loan from Manchester City. For the England playmaker it is a change of shirt that keeps him in the league he knows best, the Premier League.

Calciomercato.com confirmed the move as official, describing the operation as closed and formalised: Grealish leaves the reigning English champions to join the blue side of Liverpool. The transfer is on a loan basis, meaning his registration remains the property of Manchester City.

On the financial details, the available sources have not disclosed either the length of the loan or any additional conditions: no figures have been reported regarding a possible option or obligation to buy, the split of his wages, or bonuses. The only confirmed fact released is the official nature of the temporary move from the Citizens to the Toffees.

As things stand the deal is therefore to be considered complete on the negotiating side: Calciomercato.com lists it as official, so there are no outstanding issues for the transfer itself. Still to be learned, pending further announcements, are the precise terms of the loan, an element not yet spelled out by the sources.
