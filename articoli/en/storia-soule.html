Soulé: Borussia Dortmund get serious, the most concrete interest comes from Germany

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 16 Jun 2026

The Bundesliga is circling Roma's Argentine gem. According to Gianluca Di Marzio, Borussia Dortmund are the most concrete club on Matías Soulé: the Yellow-Blacks have tracked him for a while and remain alert to his situation. A season of 7 goals and 8 assists has turned heads.

As reported by Gianluca Di Marzio, among the several clubs to have enquired about Soulé, Borussia Dortmund's interest is the most concrete. The Germans, who have followed the former Juventus man for some time, are staying attentive to his situation with the future in mind.

The numbers explain the attention: this season the Argentine racked up 42 appearances across all competitions, garnished with 7 goals and 8 assists. The output of a wide forward who makes things happen — exactly the profile Dortmund love to develop and sell on for a premium.

Born in 2003, left-footed, used to starting from the right to drift inside and look for the shot or the final pass, Soulé came through at Juventus before moving to Roma. The lure of the Bundesliga, a historic shop window for rising talent, is obvious; it remains to be seen what stance Roma will take in the face of any offer.

For now this is about interest, not yet a negotiation: no bid on the table, but a real and lasting appreciation. Dortmund have moved to the front of the queue, and the next step will depend on how willing Roma are to open the door to a sale.
