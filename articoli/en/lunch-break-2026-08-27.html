Lunch Break: official deals galore and the Leao riddle

TransferBeat Newsroom · edited by Pierluigi Cella · Updated on 27 Aug 2026

The lunch hour serves up a menu full of signatures: from Lazio landing Pinamonti to Lecce welcoming Ilić, all the way to European moves by Barcelona and Liverpool. In the background looms the Rafa Leao puzzle, with Galatasaray and Aston Villa fighting over the Portuguese on his way out of Milan.

Serie A files away a morning of genuine signings. Lazio have made Andrea Pinamonti official, arriving from Sassuolo for 3 million euros plus bonuses, while Lecce announced Ivan Ilić on loan with an option to buy from Torino (Calciomercato.com). Two concrete additions that reshape both sides' attack and midfield.

On the «here we go» front, Samuele Ricci leaves Milan and moves to Como on a paid loan worth 1.5 million, while Giovanni Fabbian goes from Fiorentina to Parma on a paid loan with an option to buy (Calciomercato.com). Juventus, meanwhile, register Kamil Grabara from Wolfsburg: half a million on loan plus 0.3 in bonuses, as reported by Gianluca Di Marzio.

But the main course remains Rafa Leao's future. Gianluca Di Marzio writes that the Portuguese is close to leaving Milan, with Galatasaray and Aston Villa lurking in hours deemed decisive. The Villans, according to Calciomercato.com, have raised their bid: 42 million euros plus 3 in bonuses to Milan and wages of 7 million for the player.

At Napoli there is a contractual agreement in principle with Marc Guiu, with the Spanish striker pushing to play in Italy (Calciomercato.com). The outgoing market is moving too: Álvaro Morata is closing in on a loan to Leganés from Como, while Kristjan Asllani is set for Al Jazira, on loan from Inter for 2 million with an obligation to buy (Gianluca Di Marzio).

Rounding off the midday menu, from abroad comes Dominik Livaković's official move to Barcelona, signed from Fenerbahçe for 2 million, and the «here we go» for Bradley Barcola from PSG to Liverpool in a deal worth 100 million pounds (Calciomercato.com). In the background, the targets still open: Genoa are pushing for Cancellieri, Parma are sounding out Exequiel Zeballos of Boca Juniors and Monza are eyeing Como goalkeeper Noel Törnqvist.
