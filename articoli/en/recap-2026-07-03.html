DAILY RECAP · 3 Jul 2026

Provedel to Inter and Milla to Como, Milan swoop for Gila

TransferBeat Newsroom · Updated on 3 Jul 2026

Inter have wrapped up Ivan Provedel from Lazio in a EUR 3m deal, while Como made Luis Milla official from Getafe for EUR 6m on a busy day of completed transfers across Serie A, the Premier League and LaLiga. On the negotiating front Milan are pushing for Mario Gila, Inter chase Khalaili and Roma have sounded out Chelsea for Alejandro Garnacho.

The most concrete moves come in Serie A. According to Calciomercato.com, Inter have closed the deal with Lazio for Ivan Provedel in a EUR 3m operation, while Como have made Luis Milla official, signed from Getafe for EUR 6m. Also leaving Italian clubs, Fiorentina have sold Lucas Beltran to River Plate on a paid loan and Lazio have confirmed Vedat Muriqi's departure to Mallorca.

Juventus keep building on several fronts: the arrivals of goalkeeper Emiliano 'Dibu' Martinez from Aston Villa and defender Emil Muharemovic from Sassuolo are agreed, while Jeff Ekhator has arrived officially from Genoa and young goalkeeper Giovanni Daffara is heading out to Parma. Completing the Bianconeri's movements, Calciomercato.com reports David Puczka has moved to Genoa from Juventus for EUR 6m.

Milan also have two faces: after tying up Goncalo Ramos from PSG in recent days, they have waved goodbye to Samuel Chukwueze, bound for Fulham, and Suso, on his way to Sevilla. At Roma the arrival of Tresoldi from Club Brugge is official, along with the sales of Marash Kumbulla to Mallorca and Emanuele Baldanzi to Genoa; the Mason Greenwood trail stays alive, with Atletico Madrid - as reported by Calciomercato.com - sending an official bid to Marseille while the Giallorossi sit back for now.

The negotiating front is lively. Calciomercato.com flags a strong Milan move for Mario Gila, with contacts opened with the player's agent, and Inter's advance for Khalaili, for whom Union Saint-Gilloise are asking EUR 25m. Genoa are trying a loan for Artem Dovbyk, Bayern Munich have made an exploratory enquiry for Juventus' Gleison Bremer, and Torino have all but closed Gaetano Oristanio on a loan with an option to buy, adding goalkeeper Diego Mascardi.

In the Premier League, Manchester United have completed the signing of Bryan Mbeumo from Brentford, while Tottenham land a double swoop in Sandro Tonali from Newcastle and - according to Calciomercato.com - Mateus Fernandes from West Ham for GBP 85m. Manchester City have wrapped up Elliot Anderson and Bournemouth have strengthened in goal with Cristian Mandas from Lazio; in the background, Roma have asked Chelsea about Alejandro Garnacho.

In LaLiga, Atletico Madrid have reached an agreement for Morten Hjulmand from Sporting CP, while Athletic Club let Unai Gomez leave for Udinese. Several rumours swirl around Real Madrid, from the possible exits of Alex Jimenez, headed to Bournemouth, to Victor Valdepenas, tracked by Bologna. In the background the 2026 World Cup rolls on: in the round of 16 Portugal beat Croatia 2-1 thanks to Goncalo Ramos' goal set up by Rafael Leao, after Cristiano Ronaldo's temporary equaliser from the penalty spot.
